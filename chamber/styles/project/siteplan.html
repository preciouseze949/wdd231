Website Site Plan

This document outlines the purpose, design, typography, color scheme, and planned layout of the Mrpee Shoes website.

1. Site Name

Mrpee Shoes

Mrpee Shoes is a footwear brand focused on stylish, comfortable, and durable shoes. The name gives the business a distinct identity that customers can recognize and remember. It represents a brand that values craftsmanship, quality, and personal style.

Suggested domain: mrpeeshoes.com

Domain availability has not been verified.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Mrpee Shoes website is to introduce the brand, showcase its footwear collections, and provide customers with information about its products and services. Visitors will be able to browse shoe styles, learn about materials and craftsmanship, and contact the business about purchases or custom orders.

The planned website will include a homepage, a collections page, and a contact page. It will provide a responsive browsing experience on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

3. Scenarios

These questions represent information that potential customers may want to find on the website.

  • What types of shoes are available at Mrpee Shoes, and how can I view the collections?
  • How can I contact Mrpee Shoes to ask about prices or place a custom shoe order?
  • What materials are used to make the shoes, and what makes them comfortable and durable?

6. Wireframe

The wireframes will show the structure of the Mrpee Shoes homepage on small and large screens. They will be created manually in black and white, focusing on layout and content placement rather than finished styling.

Small Viewport — Mobile

The mobile layout will contain a compact header with a menu button, a hero section, featured footwear, a short brand introduction, and a footer arranged vertically.

Large Viewport — Desktop or Tablet

The larger layout will contain a horizontal navigation bar, a hero section with text and an image area side by side, featured shoes arranged in a grid, a brand introduction, and a footer.
